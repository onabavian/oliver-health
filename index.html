The drug

Weekly injection
When
Sunday evening — inject before bed so peak nausea hits during sleep
Sites — rotate every week
Abdomen (2+ in from navel) · outer thigh · upper arm
Prep
Remove from fridge 30 min before. Room temp OK up to 21 days (<86°F).

5 mgWeeks 5–8
7.5 mgWeeks 9–12 · only if tolerating well
10–15 mgOnly increase if GI is consistently fine
If GI is bad after a dose increase — stay at your current dose for 6–8 weeks instead of 4. No prize for reaching 15mg faster.
Triggers — always avoid
Fried / greasyAlcoholSpicy food Carbonated drinksLarge portionsEating too fastHigh-sugar food
When GI symptoms hit
Nausea
Zofran (Rx, as needed only). Ginger tea or chews. Small meals. Stay upright 30 min after eating.
Constipation
MiraLax (OTC, as needed). 3L water minimum. Short walk after meals.
Bloating / diarrhea
Eat simple: banana, rice, eggs. Skip cruciferous veg temporarily.
General rule
Overeating on Zepbound punishes itself. The discomfort is the feedback.

Eating out

The order — any restaurant
1
Grilled protein — chicken, fish, or lean beef
2
Simple carb — white rice or plain potato (never fries)
3
Vegetable — steamed or roasted, not creamy
★

Sunday prep

~65 min total · ~20 min hands-on · start oven and air fryer first, then stovetop while they run
Step by step
0:00Oven
Preheat to 400°F
Needs 10 min to reach temp — start here.
0:10Oven
Sweet potatoes in — 50 min
Whole, unpeeled. Score skin with a fork first.
0:10Air fryer
Chicken — 380°F, 22 min
Pat dry. Salt, pepper, garlic powder. Flip at 11 min.
0:12Stovetop
Rice — 2 cups rice, 3.5 cups water
Boil then simmer covered 18 min. Don't lift lid.
0:20Stovetop
Hard-boil 6 eggs — 10 min
Into boiling water. 10 min, then ice bath.
0:30Stovetop
Rice + eggs done — cool and set aside
Fluff rice uncovered. Eggs into ice bath 5 min.
0:32Air fryer
Chicken done — rest 5 min, store whole
Don't slice until cooled. Keeps moisture in.
0:33Cast iron
Ground beef (1 lb) — medium-high, 10 min
Break up as it cooks. Cumin + garlic powder + salt. Drain fat.
0:35Oven
Sheet pan: carrots + zucchini — 25 min
Toss in olive oil + salt. Single layer.
1:00Done
Everything out — cool and container
Cool to room temp before lidding. Salmon always fresh, never batch.
